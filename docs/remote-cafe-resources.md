## Sync

**Venue:** [https://www.instagram.com/sync.ath/](https://www.instagram.com/sync.ath/)

**Google Maps:** [Open in Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Sync+Vissis+25+Athens)

*Location:* Athens, Monastiraki (Psiri)

*Coordinates:* `37.978533`, `23.727368` (for the Workspaces map)

**Tags:** `wifi`, `power`, `all-day`, `brunch`, `coffee`

All-day **restaurant and coffee spot** on Vissis 25 (former Dope site): specialty coffee (Qahwa), brunch, and an upper **mezzanine suited for laptop work** with natural light. Evenings shift to dining and cocktails—confirm laptop policy if you stay late.
